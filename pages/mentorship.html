Before you apply

The four things people ask first.

Is this signals or copy trading?

No. You learn to read structure, place your own entries and manage your own risk. I don't send trades to copy — the point is that you can do it without me.

Do I need experience?

No. The plan is built around where you are. What matters is that you send your charts and show up to the calls.

How much time does it take?

One call a week, plus the charts you're already looking at. Chart reviews happen between calls, when you have something worth reviewing.

How do I pay?

After the short call, once we both agree it's a fit: Binance Pay or bank transfer. Nothing is charged before that.
