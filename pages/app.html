    How it works

    From a pair to a structure read without leaving the page.

    1. 01

      Enter the pair

      Type the symbol — EURUSD, XAUUSD, US30, NAS100, and more. No screenshot needed.

    2. 02

      Pick the timeframe

      Tell the scanner which candles it is reading so the read is scaled right.

    3. 03

      Get the signal

      A clear BUY or SELL call (or an honest "no trade" if nothing's clean) — plus structure, levels, entries, stop, targets and what would prove it wrong, calculated from live price bars.
